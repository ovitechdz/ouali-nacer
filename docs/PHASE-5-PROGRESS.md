## Session 2 - Batch 1 closed (2026-11-13, OpenCode)

Baseline re-measured at `90ae403`: entry **170.7 kB gzip**, lazy 128.3 kB across 46 chunks,
largest lazy chunk 27.0 kB. The session-1 numbers were confirmed exactly.

| # | Change | Entry before | Entry after | Delta | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | `farm-store` no longer imports `@/app/services` at module load; the composition root is a cached `import()` in `loadServices()`, resolved on the first store action. `quotaIssue`/`developmentSeed` take the resolved services. | 170.7 kB gzip | **132.7 kB gzip** | **-38.0 kB** | KEPT (CI green) |
| - | stop rule | - | - | 150 kB target met | Batch 1 closed |

Diagnosis: the entry carried the repository adapters, zod document parsing and the composition root
because `farm-store.ts` imported `services` eagerly. Nothing on the first-paint path needs them: the
gate shows `Splash` until `initialize()` runs. Deferring the module moves them to a separate
`services-*.js` chunk (133.2 kB raw / **37.7 kB gzip**) that the first store action loads; every lazy
page that imports `services` directly also pulls it, so there is no duplicate copy.
